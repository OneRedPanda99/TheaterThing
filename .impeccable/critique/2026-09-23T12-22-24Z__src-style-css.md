## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, not adapted -- top decile. `--work` "worklight amber"; `--gel-rose`/`--gel-congo`/`--gel-green` named for real Roscolux stock; the plan drawn from the house and captioning itself so; the blackout warmed to #2E0A00 for dark adaptation; a real print stylesheet with break-inside:avoid. The failed-call copy -- "Not sent -- the crew is still on the scene before this one" with a dismiss button reading "Call it by headset" -- cannot be written by someone who has not stood on comms.

Where it slips: the modal system is a stock centred dialog with no theatrical character, and that is where the stage manager actually spends time. Setup and the piece menu read like a different product than the deck.

Deterministic scan: 1 detector finding (layout-transition, index.html:220 = style.css:208), and it is a false positive -- the animated `--pane` is read by both the sheet and the map so they move together, `contain:layout` is set, the transition is killed during drag and above 640px. 0 true mechanical defects.

## What's Working

1. The deck's positional contract. `.deck` is `grid-template-columns:auto minmax(0,1fr)`; the crew's flat `.readout` occupies the identical slot at identical size as the SM's `.go`. A crew member mashing the biggest target in the dark cannot advance the show -- and it is a non-button, not a disabled button, because disabled buttons invite pressing.
2. Moves are derived, never authored. `movesBetween` diffs consecutive scenes, and a pivot becomes a "Turn" verb rather than a phantom reposition. This structurally eliminates the drift that kills paper run sheets.
3. Type scales with zoom, not viewport. `label()` registers a target CSS-pixel size and `sizeLabels()` multiplies by `planScale()` -- one scalar, zero width media queries, no 6px "leg 2" when zoomed out.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The failed-call alert is 1px tall in the normal running posture
`#alert` is a child of `#sheetbody`; `paintAlert` un-hides it but never changes `snap`. Measured live at the peek snap: `#sheetbody` is **1px tall** and the 60px alert renders with **1 pixel visible**.
Why it matters: the SM collapses the sheet to watch the map -- that is the whole point of the three heights. Publish fails, their device advances, twelve crew phones do not, the app knows and has written the perfect sentence about it, and the SM sees nothing. This is the exact failure the product exists to prevent, reintroduced by a layout decision.
Fix: in `paintAlert`, when `callFail !== null`, force `setSnap(1)`. Better, lift `#alert` out of `.sheetbody` into its own grid row above the deck so it is structurally un-hideable. Pair with `navigator.vibrate(200)` -- the SM may be looking at the stage.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P0] Spacebar irreversibly calls the show
`90-main.js:55`: `if(e.key==="ArrowRight"||e.key===" ") callScene(S.liveIndex+1)`. Verified live: one synthetic space keydown with focus on `<body>` advanced 4/5 -> 5/5 and fired publish. No confirm, no undo. Worse, the guard at :47 only exempts input/textarea -- so **Space on any focused button advances the show instead of pressing that button**, including the "Back" control and the alert's own "Send again".
Why it matters: the booth iPad has a keyboard. A resting script or hand jumps the show on every crew phone mid-scene, and recovery republishes again -- two wrong transitions from one keypress.
Fix: drop `" "` from the binding. If a one-key GO is wanted, require a modifier or a deliberate double-press within 400ms.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P1] Blackout mode makes every control unreadable, GO worst of all
`#dim` is a full-viewport #2E0A00 overlay at **0.78 opacity**, z-index 100 -- above modals (70) and toasts (80). Measured composites: body ink **1.86:1**, the GO label **1.63:1**, the failed-publish alert **1.46:1**. Nothing in the app reaches 2:1. Dim mode is barely better: ink 4.31, GO label 3.19. The focus ring is `var(--work)` on a `var(--work)` GO fill -- **1:1, invisible** even at full brightness.
Why it matters: the product's thesis is a thumb finding GO in the dark, and the mode built for the dark is the one that erases GO. An unconditional opacity wash degrades the critical path at exactly the same rate as decorative chrome.
Fix: dim with a floor, not a blanket. Exempt `.go`, `.alert` and the focus ring from `#dim` (raise them above z-100, or drive brightness through per-token `color-mix` so the accent keeps ~4.5:1 while the chrome falls away). Give the ring its own `--focus` token that inverts on amber fills.
Command: /impeccable audit

### [P1] The dimmer is three taps deep behind a 38px target
Reachable only via `#btn-setup` (`.btn.sm`, 38px, top-right -- the hardest corner of a one-handed phone) -> Setup modal -> a cycle button whose label is its current value, with no `aria-pressed` and no of-3 indication.
Why it matters: a crew member whose phone is too bright is spilling light into the house right now. In practice they pocket the phone, which means they stop seeing the move list, which means the app has lost. Meanwhile `#btn-replay` gets prime map real estate for a secondary action.
Fix: put the dimmer in the run deck's `.l` group as a third `.sec` at full 66px, cycling Bright -> Dim -> Blackout with the state as its `.cap`. That is 4 reachable controls in run mode -- still inside the app's own Hick's budget, and the only addition that earns a slot.
Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] No undo, and a drag commits silently
`onPointerUp` writes `sc.place[drag.id]` and calls `markDirty()` with no history. `confirmAsk` guards only deletes, not the far more frequent misdrop.
Why it matters: a fat-fingered pan that catches a piece relocates a sofa into a wing, which becomes two fabricated moves on every crew member's list at the next sync, and nothing on screen says anything happened.
Fix: keep a bounded stack of the last 10 {sceneId, pieceId, prevPlacement} and surface it in a post-drop toast -- `toast("Sofa -> Wing SR", {action:"Undo"})`. The toast machinery already exists; it needs an action slot.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P2] Nothing is ever announced, and the crew lose their place on every save
Zero `aria-live`, `role="status"` or `role="alert"` in the entire codebase. A screen-reader user on a crew phone gets **no announcement when the SM calls a scene** -- the single most important event in the app -- and none when a publish fails. Separately, `viewIdx`/`browsing` reset on every boot, so any SM save yanks a crew member who was preloading scene 9 back to live with no notice and no animation to explain it.
Fix: `role="status"` on the sheet head, `role="alert"` on `#alert`. Persist {viewIdx, browsing} to localStorage and let the existing `.jump` bar ("The show is on 5. Kitchen") do its job -- it is the right answer, it is just never given the chance to appear.
Command: /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

**First-run crew member, 15, handed a link ten minutes before house.**
- The role dialog opens with focus still on `<body>`, no `role="dialog"`, no focus trap. Pressing **Escape dismisses it, stores no role at all, and drops her silently into crew mode** -- verified live. Nothing tells her that happened or how to undo it.
- "Stage manager or director" is the first option and reads as the more important one. Nothing prevents her taking it, and then she has a full-size amber GO that republishes to everyone.
- The sheet says "On stage now / 4. Intermission". The amber block below says "Next up / 5. 2-1 Parlor". Two prominent scene numbers, and nobody has told her which is which.
- The map pinches. She will not discover this; `#btn-fit` is hidden until after she has already zoomed.

**Stage manager calling from the wing on a phone.**
- Sheet at peek so she can see the map -> the failed-call alert is the 1px band above.
- `syncChip` below 430px is a bare 7px dot; "Unsaved" and "View only" both render as the same rose dot, with the words behind a hover tooltip phones do not show.
- The Setup button she needs for the dimmer is 38px in the corner furthest from her thumb.

**Deck chief, gloves, paper sheet, phone in a back pocket.**
- Setup's show section is wrapped in `if(isSM())`, so "Print run sheet" is SM-only. **A crew member cannot print their own run sheet** -- the paper backup the README calls the answer to theatre wifi is gated behind a role they do not have.
- `pl.note` -- "upstage of leg 2", the most operationally specific text in the app -- renders as a 3px dot on the map and 12.5px at opacity .75 in the move row. Styled as the least important thing there.

## Minor Observations

- **Build mode on a phone is where the plan falls apart.** Measured 10 overlapping text pairs, including a piece-on-piece collision ("Chair A" x "Chair B") and six piece labels over zone/grid tags. Run mode suppresses captions to only the moving pieces and avoids this; build mode shows them all and gets the exact smear the code comment warns about.
- Build mode's drawing occupies **41% of its viewBox area**, so most of the map pane on a phone is empty while the plan itself renders small.
- Nine CSS rules undercut the declared `--tap:46px` floor ("nothing a thumb must hit is smaller"). Smallest is `#btn-replay` at 38x38; the alert's own "Send again" / "Call it by headset" recovery buttons are 42px.
- Set-piece handles (select, turn, menu) are SVG shapes with no role, tabindex or name -- no keyboard path to select, move or rotate a piece at all.
- No `safe-area-inset-left/right` anywhere. In landscape on a notched iPhone -- an orientation the app explicitly handles -- the topbar Setup button and the deck's "Back" sit under the notch. `.toast{bottom:110px}` is a magic number that ignores `--safe-b` and can land on GO. `.modal{max-height:86vh}` should be `dvh`.
- `.alert` hardcodes `color:#12040A` instead of a token, so it does not re-derive in dark mode. It passes by luck (6.89) but fails in light (2.91).
- `PALETTE[S.pieces.length % PALETTE.length]` means deleting then adding a piece produces a duplicate colour -- two identical `.sw` stripes in the move list defeat the swatch entirely.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured properly in both CSS and JS -- but `replay` then becomes a dead button with no feedback at all.
- `startNewShow` sets a legacy single `curtain:"closed"` key while `CURTAINS` iterates three, so new shows start with mid and scrim undefined.
- `confirmAsk` focuses the cancel button. Correct and quietly thoughtful.

## Questions to Consider

1. The app has exactly one message more important than the ground plan -- and it put that message inside the one container the user is expected to shrink to zero. What does the stage row look like if the alert is a grid row that pushes the deck up, rather than a child that hides?
2. The thesis is "a thumb finds GO in the dark." Why does the control that makes it dark live in a modal? If run mode can only afford four reachable controls, is "replay the animation" really a better use of one than "stop spilling light into the house"?
3. The README says per-crew tick-off is blocked by read-only access. But the app already owns every crew member's localStorage. What changes if "I've done my move" is purely local state that never syncs -- the list visibly shrinking to what's left? That may be the difference between an app the crew watches and one they use.
